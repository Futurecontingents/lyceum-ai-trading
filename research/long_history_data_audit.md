The longest dataset is the S&P 500 index proxy at 56.65 calendar years and 14,286 sessions. The longest directly tradeable ETF dataset is SPY at 33.58 years and 8,453 sessions. This project therefore may truthfully say “33.58 years of SPY daily evidence,” but not “33 years of executable option history.”

Auxiliary causal series are official [CBOE VIX daily history](https://cdn.cboe.com/api/global/us_indices/daily_prices/VIX_History.csv) and [FRED DGS10](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/DGS10). VIX begins in 1990; DGS10 begins in 1962. They are joined by observation date and forward-filled only after an observation exists.

## Independent reconciliation

Nasdaq's public historical endpoint supplied 667 overlapping raw-close sessions from 2024-01-02 through 2026-08-28.

| Instrument | Overlap | Median absolute close difference | Differences > $0.01 | Maximum | Result |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| QQQ | 667 | $0.0000098 | 0 | $0.0000293 | PASS |
| SPY | 667 | $0.0000146 | 1 | $1.4200 | FAIL (one unresolved source discrepancy) |

The SPY discrepancy is 2026-04-20: Yahoo raw close $708.71997 versus Nasdaq $710.14. It is retained explicitly in the manifest. No provider value was silently substituted. The strict overall independent reconciliation result is therefore **FAIL**, despite 666 of 667 SPY observations and all QQQ observations agreeing within one cent.

## Failures and limitations preserved

- Stooq's CSV endpoints returned an anti-bot proof-of-work page and were not used.
- Yahoo's `range=max` request silently returned monthly observations. That ingestion attempt was rejected; the reproducible fetch now uses explicit epoch bounds and verifies 8,453 SPY daily rows.
- `missing_weekdays_including_exchange_holidays` in the manifest is a conservative calendar diagnostic, not a claim that exchange holidays are missing data. The independent recent Nasdaq calendar has zero unmatched QQQ sessions and the single SPY issue is a value disagreement, not a missing row.
- Raw files, normalized files, credentials, local databases, and account state remain machine-local. The committed research scripts regenerate the machine artifacts.
